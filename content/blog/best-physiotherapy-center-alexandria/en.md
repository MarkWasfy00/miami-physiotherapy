## What makes a good physiotherapy center?

Successful physiotherapy doesn't depend on one machine or a fixed set of exercises for everyone. It starts with understanding the cause of the problem and how it affects your movement and daily life. At Miami Center, that means:

- Listening to your complaint and understanding your symptoms and history.
- Assessing movement, muscle strength, pain level and daily function.
- Setting clear, trackable goals for your program.
- Choosing techniques and exercises based on your assessment and diagnosis.
- Monitoring your response and adjusting the plan as needed.
- Providing a comfortable environment with private treatment sessions.

## Our services

### Bone and joint problems

Programs for disc herniation, spinal and joint arthritis, tendinitis, ligament tears, and knee and shoulder injuries. Each program begins with an assessment of movement, strength and pain.

### Neurological rehabilitation

Rehab for spinal cord injuries, paralysis of all types, post-stroke recovery, peripheral nerve injuries and walking or balance problems — focused on motor control, strength and daily function.

### Disc herniation

Programs may include therapeutic exercise, manual therapy and 3D spinal traction where appropriate — traction isn't suitable for every case and is decided after examination.

### Supporting techniques

Depending on your assessment, your plan may include:

- **Manual therapy** to improve joint and tissue mobility and reduce stiffness.
- **Electrotherapy** to stimulate nerves and muscles.
- **Ultrasound therapy** to support tissue flexibility and recovery.
- **Shockwave therapy** for certain chronic tendon and muscle problems.
- **Traction** to relieve pressure on the spine in suitable cases.
- **Cryotherapy** to ease pain and swelling after some acute injuries.
- **TECAR and low-level laser** therapy as part of a specialist plan.

## Tips before your first visit

- Book in advance to secure a convenient time.
- Bring any reports, scans and previous prescriptions.
- Wear comfortable clothing.
- Follow your therapist's instructions and home program.

Remember that the exact plan and number of sessions can't be set before your assessment, as every patient differs in diagnosis, symptoms and response to treatment.
